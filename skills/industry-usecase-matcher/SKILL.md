# Industry Use Case Matcher

## Overview
The `industry-usecase-matcher` skill navigates the comprehensive catalog of 500+ AI agent projects to surface proven design patterns across enterprise sectors.

## Supported Sectors
- **Finance & FinTech:** Quantitative equity research, SEC EDGAR filing extractors, fraud anomaly detection.
- **Healthcare & Clinical:** Medical literature synthesis, clinical trial matching, patient inquiry triage.
- **Cybersecurity & IT Operations:** Log triage, automated vulnerability remediation, incident response playbooks.
- **E-Commerce & Retail:** Personalized shopping concierges, dynamic pricing monitors, customer support deflection.
- **Legal & Compliance:** Contract redlining, regulatory change monitoring, PII redacting pipelines.

## Execution Rules
- Prioritize production-tested implementations with reproducible benchmark results.
- Always provide direct links to the relevant reference agent directory or repository guide.
